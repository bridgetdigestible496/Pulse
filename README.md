## 🛠️ Customizing Pulse

Pulse works great right away, but you can tailor it to your style.

### Change the Hotkey

Right-click the lightning bolt icon in your system tray, select **Settings**, and then click on the shortcut field. Press any key combination you like (for example, **Ctrl + Shift + P** or **Double-Tap Ctrl**). Click **Save**.

### Add Your Favorite Apps

Pulse automatically scans your Start Menu and common app folders when you first launch it. To make sure a specific app always appears at the top of your results, open Pulse's settings and click **Favorites**. Click **Add Program**, then browse for any `.exe` file on your computer.

### Dark or Light Theme

Go to **Settings → Appearance** and pick **Dark Mode** or **Light Mode**. Pulse remembers your choice the next time you start it.

### Startup Behavior

Do you want Pulse to start every time you turn on your PC? In **Settings**, check the box labeled **Start with Windows**. This ensures your launcher is always ready when you are.

## 📝 Troubleshooting Basics

If Pulse does not open when you press your hotkey, try these steps in order:

1. **Check the system tray** — make sure the lightning bolt icon is visible. If not, look in the hidden icons area (the up-arrow near the clock).
2. **Right-click the icon** — select **Show Pulse** to force it to display.
3. **Verify the hotkey** — open Settings and set a new shortcut.
4. **Restart Pulse** — right-click the icon, select **Exit**, then launch Pulse again.
5. **Run as administrator** — if Pulse will not open certain apps, right-click the Pulse shortcut and choose **Run as administrator**.
